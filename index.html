Plan your posts in one place

Schedora is a Windows app for planning and scheduling your own social media posts. It runs on your computer and keeps your schedule there.

What it does

  • Plans posts on a calendar, one at a time or many at once from a CSV file.
  • Posts to the accounts you connect while the app is running, or reminds you to post.
  • Keeps the channels of different brands apart.
  • Uses images and videos from your computer or from web links you choose.

With TikTok

You log in on TikTok’s own page. Schedora shows which account is connected and, when a video is due, sends it to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You finish and post it in the TikTok app.

With Pinterest

You log in on Pinterest’s own page. Schedora lists your boards so you can pick one, and creates the Pins you schedule.

Schedora is made for its owner’s own accounts and isn’t offered as a download. It works with TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, X and Bluesky accounts you own or manage.
